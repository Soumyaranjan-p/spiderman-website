Manual & FAQs

How it works

1. Setup: Open the app from your Windows system tray (bottom right corner).

2. Set the Time: Type your reminder text and pick the exact time you want it to trigger.

3. The Drop: At the set time, Spiderman will physically drop down from the top of your screen to deliver the message.

Installing on Windows

1. Download WebbitReminderSetup 1.0.0.exe and run it.

2. If SmartScreen appears, click More info, then Run anyway.

3. Follow the setup wizard — Webbit will appear in your system tray once installed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it completely free?

Yes! Webbit is 100% free forever with no hidden costs or ads.

Does it work on Windows?

Yes — Webbit runs on Windows 10 and above.

Can I set recurring reminders?

Yes, you can configure it to repeat daily from the tray settings.
